## 🎯 The Sincere Journey: From Education to Tech Support

I began my academic career earning a **Bachelor of Education in English** at Yala Rajabhat University. During my studies and teaching practice, I discovered three foundational strengths:
1. **Patience & Empathy:** Translating complex, intimidating concepts into simple, welcoming explanations for students of all backgrounds.
2. **High-Stress Calmness:** Managing crowded classrooms, strict competition timelines, and parental communication with calm composure.
3. **Curiosity for Systems & Data:** Tracking student attendance, test score distribution, and learning bottlenecks using spreadsheets.

### 💡 The Pivot into Technology
Inspired by close family members working in the IT and software industry, I realized my communication skills and methodical mindset were a natural match for **remote technical support and data operations**.

In 2024, I applied for and won a competitive scholarship for the **DataRockie Data Science Bootcamp (Batch 12)** taught by Kasidis Satangmongkol. I completed over 100 hours of intensive coursework covering:
* Relational Databases & SQL querying
* Data cleaning, Regular Expressions, and Spreadsheet automation
* Fundamentals of R, Python, and statistical modeling
* Ranked in the **Top 20% Mastered** across 240 comprehensive evaluation questions.

## 🤝 What I Bring to a Technical Support Team

### 1. Fluent, Empathetic English Communication
I communicate comfortably in written and spoken English with international users, engineers, and product managers. I can de-escalate frustrated users by validating their concerns and providing crystal-clear troubleshooting steps.

### 2. Analytical Triage Mindset
When a user submits a ticket saying *"The system is broken,"* I don't guess. I systematically isolate the problem:
* Is it a browser/cache issue or an API outage?
* Does the database record show incorrect user roles or missing foreign keys?
* Can the issue be reproduced with specific payloads?

### 3. High Stamina for Ticket Queues
Teaching 40+ energetic students daily taught me that consistency, stamina, and patience are non-negotiable. I welcome high ticket queues and take pride in meeting first-response and resolution SLAs.

### 4. Transparent & Coachable
I am honest about what I know and what I am currently learning. I take feedback constructively, document solutions in internal knowledge bases, and ramp up quickly on company-specific tools.
