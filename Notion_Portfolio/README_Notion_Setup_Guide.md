## ⚡ How to Build & Publish on Notion in 15 Minutes

### Step 1: Open Your Notion Workspace
1. Go to your Notion account or your live page: `https://chip-curler-7a4.notion.site`.
2. Create a top-level page or update your existing home page.

### Step 2: Configure Page Settings (Crucial for Modern Look)
1. In the top right corner of Notion, click the three dots (`...`).
2. Toggle on **Full width** (makes tables and code blocks look spacious and professional).
3. Set Font style to **Default** or **Sans-Serif**.
4. Click **Add cover** $\rightarrow$ choose a sleek, minimal dark or abstract banner image.
5. Click **Add icon** $\rightarrow$ choose `💻` or `⚡`.

### Step 3: Copy-Paste Markdown Directly
* Notion natively supports Markdown!
* Simply open any file in this folder (e.g. `00_Master_Hub_Page.md`), copy the text, and paste it directly onto a Notion page. Notion will instantly convert:
  * Headers (`#`, `##`, `###`)
  * Tables (`| ... |`)
  * Bullet points and numbered lists
  * Bold text and links

### Step 4: Add Native Notion Visual Blocks
* **Callout Boxes:** Type `/callout` to convert key quotes and bios into attractive colored boxes.
* **Code Blocks:** Type `/code` and set the language dropdown to `SQL` or `Python` for syntax highlighting.
* **Toggle Lists:** Type `/toggle` for step-by-step query explanations so visitors can expand and collapse sections.
* **Sub-pages:** Create sub-pages for Case Studies 1, 2, and 3, then drag them into a Gallery View or List block.

### Step 5: Publish & Share with Hiring Managers
1. Click the **Share** button at the top right of your Notion page.
2. Under the **Publish** tab, toggle on **Publish to web**.
3. Copy your public link: `https://chip-curler-7a4.notion.site/...`
4. Paste this link into:
   * Your CV/Resume under the Projects section.
   * Your LinkedIn profile featured links.
   * Your email job applications (as already pre-formatted in [`Cover_Letters/Email_Submission_L1_Support.md`](../Cover_Letters/Email_Submission_L1_Support.md)).
