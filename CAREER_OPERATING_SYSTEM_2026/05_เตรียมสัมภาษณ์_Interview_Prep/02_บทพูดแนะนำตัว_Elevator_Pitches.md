# Elevator Pitches ("Tell me about yourself")

Choose the pitch that matches the job you are interviewing for!

## 1. The Data Analyst Pitch (60 Seconds)
"Hi, I'm Nurlan. I recently became a Certified Professional Data Analyst through the DataRockie Bootcamp, where I built a strong foundation in SQL, Python, R, and data visualization using Tableau. But my background is actually quite unique—I spent the last four years working as an English Teacher and Coordinator. In education, I constantly had to analyze student performance metrics to adapt my teaching strategies and improve test scores, which sparked my passion for working with data. Because I'm a teacher, my biggest strength as an analyst is my ability to take complex, technical data and explain it simply and clearly to people who aren't technical. I'm looking for a role where I can combine my technical data skills with my strong communication skills to help drive business decisions."

## 2. The English Teacher/Coordinator Pitch (60 Seconds)
"Hi, I'm Nurlan. I'm a dedicated English Teacher with a degree in Education from Yala Rajabhat University and a professional teaching license. For the past few years, I've been teaching secondary students at the Darul Ma'arif Foundation, where I recently coached my students to win gold medals in provincial academic competitions. I specialize in making learning interactive, whether that's through games for young learners or presentation skills for older students. In addition to teaching, I'm highly organized—I previously served as the Vice President of the university Archery Club, coordinating international events and managing documents. I'm looking for a role where I can continue to innovate in the classroom and help students reach their full potential."

## 3. The 30-Second "Networking" Pitch (For LinkedIn/Events)
"Hi, I'm Nurlan. I'm a certified Data Analyst with a professional background in education. I specialize in using tools like SQL and Python to uncover insights, and I use my background as a teacher to present those insights in a way that anyone can understand. I'm currently looking for opportunities in the data space where communication is just as important as the code."
