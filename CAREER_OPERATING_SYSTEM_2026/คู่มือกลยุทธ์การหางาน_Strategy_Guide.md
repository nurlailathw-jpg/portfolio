# CAREER OPERATING SYSTEM: STRATEGY GUIDE 🚀

Welcome to your central command for job hunting! Since you have dual expertise in **Education** and **Data Analysis**, we need a targeted strategy.

## 🎯 Weekly Targets
- **Applications:** Send out 5-10 highly tailored applications per week.
- **Networking:** Reach out to 2 alumni from DataRockie or Yala Rajabhat University on LinkedIn.
- **Skill Building:** Spend 3 hours/week building a new Data Dashboard or writing a blog post.

## 📅 Daily Routine
1. **Morning (30 mins):** Check LinkedIn and job boards for new postings. Save interesting ones as PDFs in the `02_Active_Applications` folder.
2. **Mid-day (1 hour):** Tailor your resume for 1-2 specific roles using your `Master_Resume_Extended`.
3. **Afternoon (30 mins):** Update the `Job_Hunting_Tracker` and follow up on applications sent 1-2 weeks ago.

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Your Background
* **When applying for Data roles:** Emphasize your DataRockie bootcamp, your R Project, and how your teaching background makes you excellent at communicating complex data to non-technical stakeholders (a very rare and valuable skill!).
* **When applying for Education/Coordination roles:** Emphasize your teaching license, your leadership in the Archery Club, and your ability to use data to track student performance.
